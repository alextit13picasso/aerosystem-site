We build simple, bulletproof automation systems that help service businesses capture inquiries, respond faster, and manage new customers without standard manual work. No complex software, no tech debt—just operations running on autopilot.

Core Automation Services

1. Lead Capture Automation: We sync your Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, and website forms directly with your internal calendar or CRM. Every client inquiry from social media instantly creates an open ticket, preventing lost messages in cluttered DMs.

2. Instant Customer Notifications: When a new booking or inquiry arrives, the system immediately drops a direct confirmation to the client via SMS or WhatsApp, while dispatching a push notification to your team's device with the job details, location, and schedule slot.

3. Automated Follow-Ups: We set up hands-free post-service workflows. The system automatically requests text reviews on Google/Yelp 2 hours after a job is completed and triggers scheduled reminders for recurring services (laundry cycles, detailing routines, seasonal window washing).
